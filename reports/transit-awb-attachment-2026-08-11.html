Bug — wrong success dialog assumed

"تقديم الطلب" doesn't show the usual confirmation dialog

clickSaveAndSubmit() on step 1 shows a هل ترغب بتقديم المانيفست؟ confirmation before submitting. Step 2's تقديم الطلب button was assumed to behave the same way — it doesn't. It submits immediately and shows the app's standard تنبيه من فسح success popup directly, the same "vx1 modal show" pattern already used elsewhere in this codebase.

First run failed waiting on the wrong dialog, even though the manifest itself had already submitted successfully (reference number was visible in the failure snapshot). Replaced the wait with a dedicated submitSuccessAlert locator and getSubmittedReferenceNumberAndClose(). Re-run confirmed clean.
