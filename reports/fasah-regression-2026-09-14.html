Dominant cause

Login OTP challenge — 12 of 32 tests that ever failed (37%)

At some point during a login, the portal asked for a one-time password instead of completing sign-in. LoginPage.loginToApplication() has no OTP step, so the test just sat there until its action timeout. It didn't hit the same tests consistently — several tests passed on one attempt and hit the OTP wall on the other — which points to a rate/volume trigger rather than a per-account block.

TimeoutError: locator.fill: Timeout 45000ms exceeded. Call log: - waiting for locator('input[name="otp"]')

This run went in with 6 concurrent Playwright sessions logging in and out all day — 5 regression buckets plus a live debugging session on the same accounts, on top of dozens of earlier scratch-test logins in this same session. That's very likely what triggered it. Not a code bug — re-run the 12 flagged tests alone, spaced out, before treating any of them as broken.

Worth a look

Same warehouse-code field times out on fill — 4 tests, all import-manifest "waiting bill" flows

Unlike the OTP wall, this one repeats on the exact same locator every time, across four otherwise-unrelated import-manifest tests that all add a bill while its manifest is still in "قيد الانتظار". Concentrated enough that it's worth its own investigation pass, separate from the OTP noise above.
